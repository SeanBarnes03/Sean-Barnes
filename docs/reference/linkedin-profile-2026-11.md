## Summary

For more than 20 years, I've helped leaders close the gap between strategy and execution by aligning people, processes, and resources behind their most important objectives. Whether advising executives, leading enterprise transformation, or commanding elite organizations, my focus has remained the same: create alignment, build trust, and deliver measurable results. Today, I serve as a Senior Project Manager for Enterprise Operations at NeoGenomics Laboratories. I lead enterprise initiatives that advance organizational transformation, operational improvement, and strategic footprint optimization. I partner across business functions to align diverse stakeholders, improve executive decision-making, and turn strategic priorities into valuable outcomes.

Before entering the private sector, I spent more than two decades as a U.S. Marine Corps officer leading organizations across the Indo-Pacific, Middle East, and Washington, D.C. I commanded the Marine Corps' only forward-deployed reconnaissance battalion, advised senior leaders on enterprise-level operational challenges, and led multinational organizations during combat operations, humanitarian missions, and diplomatic engagements. That experience strengthened my appreciation for resilience, perspective, and servant leadership, principles that continue to shape how I lead today.

I'm currently pursuing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, expanding my expertise in corporate finance, capital allocation, enterprise strategy, and value creation as I prepare for broader executive leadership opportunities. My long-term interests include enterprise strategy, organizational transformation, corporate development, and helping leadership teams solve complex business challenges while creating sustainable organizational value. I welcome any opportunity to connect with leaders who are passionate about strategy, transformation, operational excellence, and building high-performing organizations.
