# NEO overhead: brief

**Scope.** Expand two slides of `NEO_Cost_Breakdown__Savings_v2`. On slide 5, break down corporate
G&A in as much detail as the filings allow. On slide 6, explain why NeoGenomics' SG&A (48.5% of
sales) is about three times Quest's and Labcorp's.

**Hypothesis.** Most of the gap is structural, from scale and the specialty-oncology model, plus
accounting choices: depreciation, amortization and stock pay booked in G&A. Only a minority is
cuttable excess, and the right benchmark is specialty labs, not Quest and Labcorp.

**What would disprove it.** NEO's G&A, normalized to recurring cash, still sitting well above
specialty peers of similar size.

**Result.** Partly confirmed. NEO's G&A is the highest of the specialty peers even after
normalizing, but the recurring cash gap is about 4 points (~$27M), not 30.
