# Sean Barnes

## Who I Am

Sean is a Senior Project Manager at NeoGenomics Laboratories and a retired U.S. Marine Corps Lieutenant Colonel with more than two decades of leadership and operational experience. A native of Brockton, Massachusetts, Sean earned a Bachelor of Science in Criminal Justice from Northeastern University before beginning his Marine Corps career.

Throughout his military service, Sean led teams and organizations in complex environments across the United States and overseas, including Japan, Iraq, Afghanistan, and the Middle East. He commanded 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion and later served as the Marine Forces Central Command Inspector General before retiring from active duty in December 2024. These experiences shaped his ability to lead diverse teams, develop strategy, and make sound decisions in complex and high-pressure environments.

Today, Sean brings that leadership experience to the private sector through his work in project management at NeoGenomics Laboratories. He is pursuing the University of Hawaiʻi DLEMBA to deepen his business acumen, broaden his perspective, and complement his leadership experience with formal business education. His long-term goal is to integrate leadership and business expertise to drive strategic decision-making, solve complex organizational challenges, and create sustainable value for the organizations and people he serves.
