## Estimates (method)

- Stock pay in G&A = 84% nine-month share × $41.3M = $34.7M; in S&M ≈ $2.9M.
- Depreciation in G&A = $67.9M non-cash − $34.7M − $12.9M = $20.3M.
- Functional split of the $176M core: sized from the 10-K drivers and typical specialty-lab cost shares. Indicative only.
- Bridge to Quest: routine-lab selling cost assumed ~4.5% of sales; ~$36M fixed public-company core; revenue-cycle cost ~7% vs ~3%. The residual is what remains.
