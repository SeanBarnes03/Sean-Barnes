Where NEO sits on the cost-volume-profit chart

FY2025 annual CVP (left) and the quarterly cost data behind the split (right)

Why overhead matters here: with a 28.5% CM ratio, each $1M of fixed cost needs $3.5M of sales to cover it. Cutting the ~$28–50M G&A gap would lower GAAP break-even by ~$100–175M of sales.
Two levers, both needed: grow sales (each +10% lifts adj. EBITDA ~48%) and raise the CM ratio through lab supply and test-mix gains. Sales growth alone needs ~$960M to reach GAAP profit.
