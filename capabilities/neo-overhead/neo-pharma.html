Non-clinical: resize now, decide by Q1 2027

Executive summary · NeoGenomics pharma services and other non-clinical revenue

What the filings show

  • Non-clinical revenue (pharma services, data, UK lab, Trapelo until sold) fell from $96M (2023) to $61M over the last 12 months.
  • Still falling: -11% from Q4’25 to Q3’26. Smaller year-on-year declines mostly reflect a smaller base.
  • Not just the market: Labcorp’s pharma services grew 4% organically in 2025; NEO’s non-clinical fell 24%.
  • Fragile revenue: most pharma contracts can be ended by the client immediately or on notice.

What has to be proven to keep it

  • Trial work turns into clinical revenue (no public evidence yet).
  • RaDaR has a funded trial path to reimbursement.
  • The unit covers the costs that would leave with it: $9–30M, depending on its margin.
  • Revenue has stopped falling: orders ≥ revenue billed for two quarters.
