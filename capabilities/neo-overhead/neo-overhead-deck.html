Executive summary

NEO corporate overhead: what’s in it and why it runs above peers · FY2025, Q3 2026 where noted

1 · What’s in G&A (slide 5)

  • $273.3M, 37.6% of sales, but only $176M is the day-to-day back office.
  • $68M is non-cash: stock pay $34.3M, depreciation $20.8M, amortization $12.8M.
  • $22M is “one-time” (Natera litigation, Pathline deal, CEO change) and $7.3M is executive and board cash pay.
  • G&A dollars rose 24% since 2021; the ratio fell only because sales rose 50%. People (+$11.5M) and software (+$8.0M) drove 2025.

2 · Why it’s above peers (slide 6)

  • vs. Quest: 30.6 pts ($223M). 10.2 pts is stock pay, one-offs and depreciation; 20.4 pts is recurring cash overhead.
  • vs. specialty labs, total SG&A is low (50.2% vs. 55.4% median) because NEO spends little on selling.
  • G&A is the outlier: 31.1% of sales like for like vs. a 24.3% peer median: 6.8 pts, ~$50M ($28M ex one-offs).
  • Behind it: four acquisitions, 80 days to collect vs. 48 at Quest, 83% of stock pay landing in G&A, CEO turnover, headcount +14% vs. sales +10%.

Six reasons NEO’s overhead runs high

What the filings show behind the gap

1 · Four deals, never one company

Clarient (2015), Genoptix (2018), Inivata (2021), Pathline (2025). Overlapping labs, systems and teams. “One-time” costs every year: $26.1M in 2024, $49.8M in 2025.

2 · Slow collections

80 days of sales sit in receivables vs. 48 at Quest and a 46-day specialty median. Only Fulgent (96) is worse. The billing staff who chase it sit in G&A.

3 · Stock pay concentrated in G&A

Total stock pay is low for a specialty lab (5.7% vs. 10.4% median), but 83% of it lands in G&A vs. 38–63% at peers: it goes to executives and admin, not sales or R&D.

4 · Leadership churn

CEO changed in 2025 (Smith to Zook) and changes again in January 2027 (Stone). 2025 carried $3.5M of transition costs and $7.7M of pay to two departed executives.

5 · Systems spend rising

Software & technology +$8.0M in 2025, the biggest non-pay increase in G&A. Running old and new platforms side by side costs double until the old ones are switched off.

6 · People ahead of sales

Headcount ~2,200 → ~2,500 (+14%) while sales grew 10%. G&A pay +$11.5M. Plus $11.3M of Natera patent litigation to defend RaDaR.
