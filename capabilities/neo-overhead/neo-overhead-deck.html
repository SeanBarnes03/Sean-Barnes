NEO’s overhead: what’s in it and why it’s high

Deep dive on v2 slides 5 and 6 · FY2025, updated with Q3 2026 where noted

$273M of G&A = 37.6% of sales
Only $176M is the day-to-day back office (billing, IT, finance, HR, offices, fees). The rest is non-cash charges ($68M), “one-time” items ($22M) and leadership pay ($7M).
30.7 pts behind Quest
SG&A of 48.5% vs. 17.8% is a $223M/yr gap. About a third is accounting and one-offs; about half is the cost of being a small, specialty oncology lab. The rest is plain excess.
Wrong yardstick, partly
Against specialty labs (Myriad, Exact, Castle, Fulgent) NEO’s total SG&A is low. Its problem is G&A alone: 37.6% vs. a 27.4% peer median, the highest of the group.
Real cash gap: ~4 pts, ~$27M
Strip out stock pay, depreciation, amortization and one-offs and NEO’s cash G&A is 25.2% of sales, vs. ~21% at Castle. That is the size of the prize, and it matches the ~$24M in the v2 deck.
☉
Why it’s high: four acquisitions never fully folded together, a billing operation that waits ~93 days to get paid (Quest: ~48), stock pay at 7× the big-lab rate, three CEOs in four years, and a public-company cost base spread over just $727M of sales.
Sources: NeoGenomics FY2025 10-K, Q3 2025 10-Q, Q4 2025 and Q3 2026 results; peer FY2025 10-Ks and earnings releases. Figures marked EST are our estimates; method on each slide and in the notes page. Not investment advice.

Six reasons NEO’s overhead runs high

What the filings show behind the gap

1 · Four deals, never one company

Clarient (2015), Genoptix (2018), Inivata (2021), Pathline (2025). Six US labs, overlapping systems and teams. Deal and integration costs show up every year: $26M of “one-time” items in 2024, $50M in 2025.

2 · A slow, heavy billing machine

~93 days to get paid vs. ~48 at Quest, and getting worse (from ~80). Oncology tests need prior auth and appeals across thousands of payers; billing staff sit in G&A.

3 · Stock pay at 7× the big labs

$41.3M, 5.7% of sales vs. 0.8% at Quest and Labcorp; 79% of executive pay is equity. ~$35M of it lands in G&A.

4 · Leadership churn

Three CEOs since 2022 and a fourth (Warren Stone) from January 2027. 2025 carried $3.5M of transition costs and $7.7M of pay to two departed executives.

5 · Systems spend rising

Software & technology +$8.0M in 2025, the biggest non-pay increase. Running old and new platforms side by side costs double until the old ones are switched off.

6 · People ahead of sales

Headcount ~2,200 → ~2,500 (+14%) while sales grew 10%. G&A pay +$11.5M. Plus $11.3M of Natera patent litigation to defend RaDaR.
